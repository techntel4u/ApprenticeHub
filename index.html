What is e-track?

e-track is your online portfolio. It keeps everything about your apprenticeship in one place.

You'll also see it called Maytas Hub. You, your assessor and your employer all see the same record. You use it to show what you've learned, keep track of how you're doing, and stay in touch with your assessor.

Log in to e-track

e-track works in any web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There's nothing to install.

Open e-track

1. Set up MFA first

You must set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your college account before you can sign in. Follow the college guides in order:

  1. Part 1: Set up MFA (PDF)
  2. Part 2: Create a passkey (PDF)
  3. Part 3: Log in using MFA (PDF)

2. Sign in with Microsoft

On the login page, click Sign in with Microsoft and use your college email and password. Approve the sign-in on your phone.

Don't type your college details into the Username and Password boxes. Those are for staff and employers.

Forgotten your password? Reset it at passwordreset.microsoftonline.com using the password reset guide (PDF).

On your phone? Open the address in your browser and add it to your home screen, so e-track opens in one tap like an app. Show me how

Lost or changed your phone, or can't sign in? Contact the IT Helpdesk at servicedesk@wolvcoll.ac.uk.

What you'll use e-track for

The six things you'll do most.

Submit your evidence

Upload your work and tick the knowledge, skills and behaviours (KSBs) it shows.

Check your progress

See how far you are compared with where you should be, and what still needs evidence.

Complete your reviews

Fill in and sign your progress reviews with your assessor and employer.

Log off-the-job hours

Record your training as you go, and see how many hours you have left to do.

Message your assessor

Ask questions and get feedback. Messages stay on your record, so nothing gets lost.

Read your feedback

See what your assessor thinks of your work, and what to do next.

Words you'll see in e-track

What the terms mean.

KSBs
Knowledge, skills and behaviours: what your apprenticeship standard says you need to show.
Evidence
Work that shows what you've learned, such as a written piece, photos, a video or a witness statement.
Mapping
Ticking the KSBs your evidence shows. Your assessor checks your choices.
Gap analysis
The Mappings screen. It shows which KSBs have evidence and which still need some.
Progress review
A regular meeting with your assessor and employer to talk about how you're doing and agree targets.
Off-the-job training (OTJ)
Learning in your paid working hours that's part of your apprenticeship but separate from your normal work.
MFA
Multi-factor authentication: approving your sign-in on your phone, so only you can get into your account.
Gateway and EPA
Gateway is when you're ready for your end-point assessment (EPA), the final independent assessment of your apprenticeship.
